Marty McFly's watch: what most fans get wrong

Watches on Screen · planned Launch #5 · manual post after warm-up · 8 slides

Caption

Quick test for Back to the Future fans: which Casio does Marty wear? ⌚⚡

Most people say CA-53W, but in the 1985 original it's the CA-50. The CA-53W only appears in Part II and Part III. And in 2025, for the film's 40th anniversary, Casio released a special calculator watch inspired by the DeLorean.

Did you know? Be honest 👇

Sources: Wikipedia (Casio CA-53W); Casio; the film.
