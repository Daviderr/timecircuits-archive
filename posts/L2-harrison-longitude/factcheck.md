## Required fixes
- Caption: "Thousands of sailors died because nobody could tell time at sea." → "Thousands of sailors died because nobody could fix a ship's position at sea." (or "...partly because nobody could tell time at sea.")
- Slide 2: "The navigators simply didn't know where they were." → "Bad charts, faulty compasses, and no way to fix longitude: the navigators didn't know where they were."

## Optional improvements
- Slide 5: H1 is dated 1735, but its trial was in 1736. "It works on its first trial" is fine. You could add "in 1736" for precision.
- Slide 7: the voyage began in November 1761. Consider year "1761" or "1761–62", since the slide is about the 81 days at sea.
- Caption: after "never awarded to anyone", consider adding "though Harrison was eventually paid over £23,000" so the ending doesn't feel like a total injustice.
- Slide 7: Wikipedia says "5 seconds" and other sources say 5.1 s. Either is fine.

Summary: All dates, numbers, names and quotes check out. The one problem is the causal claim for the 1707 disaster: the caption (and slide 2 by implication) blames it purely on timekeeping/longitude, which historians dispute. Fix those two lines and the post is ready.
