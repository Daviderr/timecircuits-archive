Thousands of sailors died because nobody could fix a ship's position at sea. ⚓

In 1714 Britain offered a fortune to whoever solved the longitude problem. Everyone expected an astronomer. Instead, a self-taught carpenter spent decades building ever-better sea clocks, and his H4 changed navigation forever. Officially, the Longitude Prize was never awarded to anyone, though Harrison was eventually paid over £23,000.
