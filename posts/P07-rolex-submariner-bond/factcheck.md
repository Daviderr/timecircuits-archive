## Required fixes
- Slide 2: "Then, for the next eight books, **the brand disappears.**" → "Then, for the next eight books, **Bond doesn't wear one.**"
- Caption: "Sources: WatchTime (2009); NATO; Phillips; Wikipedia." → "Sources: WatchTime (2009); NATO; New Atlas; Bob's Watches; Wikipedia."

## Optional improvements
- Slide 3: think about calling 1958 a reaffirmation rather than a reversal, e.g. title "Fleming *says no*". Or keep "changes his mind" and add a nod to 1963, when *OHMSS* gives Bond back a Rolex Oyster Perpetual as a knuckle-duster. That would also close the slide 2 loop nicely.
- Slide 4: "buys his first Rolex" → "buys his own Rolex, an Explorer" is a bit safer, since the source dates the Explorer but doesn't spell out "first".
- Add the jamesbondwatches.com letter page as a source for the exact 1958 quote. The WatchTime PDF doesn't extract as text.

Summary: The content is solid and well hedged (the Broccoli legend and the NATO strap myth are handled correctly). There are two errors: the brand doesn't disappear from the novels (a villain wears a Rolex in *Thunderball*), and the caption cites Phillips, which isn't a listed source. Fix both and it's ready to publish.
