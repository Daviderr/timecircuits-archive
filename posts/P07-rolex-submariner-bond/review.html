Caption

Ian Fleming gave James Bond a Rolex in 1954. Four years later, he said Bond preferred cheap watches. 🍸⌚

Then Dr. No arrived in 1962, and Sean Connery's Submariner 6538 turned a tool watch into a legend. How it got onto his wrist is still disputed, and where it went is still unknown.
