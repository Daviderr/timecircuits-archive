## Required fixes
- Slide 3 title: "The Swiss got there *first*" → "The Swiss were *ahead*" (body can stay as is).
- Caption: "the Swiss had working quartz prototypes first, and they beat Seiko's in 1967" → "the Swiss had working quartz prototypes too, and they beat Seiko's in 1967".
- Slide 4: "Price: 450,000 yen, more than a Toyota Corolla." → "Price: 450,000 yen, about as much as a Toyota Corolla."
- Slide 6 title: "1,600 watchmakers become *600*" → "1,600 watch firms become *600*" (the figure counts companies, 1970–1983, not people).
- post.json sources: the Corolla comparison is not on the cited Seiko 50th page; add https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astron_(wristwatch) (medium-sized car / Corolla E10 ¥432,000) and a source for the 1967 placings (https://omegaprototypes.com/beta-21/).

## Optional improvements
- Slide 1: "The watch that almost killed…" could be "The technology that almost killed…" — the Astron sold in tiny numbers; cheap mass quartz did the damage. Fine as a hook if kept.
- Slide 2: "perfectly steady rate" → "remarkably steady rate" (quartz drifts slightly with temperature).
- Slide 7: Swatch figure is conservative; could keep as is (safe) — no change needed.

Summary: All dates, prices, counts and the 1967 Neuchâtel result check out; no outright errors.
Four wording fixes needed: "Swiss first" (both had 1967 prototypes), "more than a Corolla" (sources say about equal), and "watchmakers" meaning companies.
