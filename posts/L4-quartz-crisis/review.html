The Quartz Crisis

Horology History · planned Launch #4 · manual post after warm-up · 9 slides

Caption

In 1969 a watch with a battery nearly ended centuries of Swiss watchmaking. ⚡

The twist: the Swiss had working quartz prototypes too, and they beat Seiko's in 1967. But Seiko put one on sale first. Within 15 years, two out of three Swiss watch jobs were gone, until a plastic watch called Swatch changed everything.
