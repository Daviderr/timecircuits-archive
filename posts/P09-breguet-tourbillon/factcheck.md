## Required fixes
- Slide 5: "Breguet fits his first tourbillon regulator into a chronometer by his late friend John Arnold, the English maker, and gives it to Arnold's son." → "Breguet gives Arnold's son a chronometer by his late friend John Arnold, fitted with what its plaque calls *'the first tourbillon regulator by Breguet.'* **It's now in the British Museum.**" (keep year 1808 as the gift date)

## Optional improvements
- Slide 6 (No. 160): this watch has **no tourbillon**. In a tourbillon post, and placed after the 1808 slide, readers may assume it has one. Consider a short bridge, e.g. "His most famous watch has no tourbillon at all…". The timeline also jumps back (1808 → 1783), so think about moving S6–S7 before S3 or adding that bridge.
- Cover: soften the hook a little ("a problem your watch *barely has*"), or leave it as is and rely on S8's hedge.
- Caption sources: add "Swatch Group archive" (it is the source for the 4/6-minute claim).
- Check the image credits (#16) before publishing.

Summary: Facts are well sourced. Patent date, the 35 sold, the 4/6-minute cages, No. 160 and the 1983 theft all check out.
One required fix: Slide 5 presents 1808 (the gift year) as the year the tourbillon was fitted and states "first" as fact. Attribute "first" to the plaque.
