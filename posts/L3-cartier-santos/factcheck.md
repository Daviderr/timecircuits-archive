# Fact-check: Cartier Santos: the watch made for a man who flew
Verdict: PASS WITH FIXES

## Required fixes
- Slide 4: "Santos-Dumont told Cartier that **checking the time in flight was nearly impossible.**" → "As the story goes, Santos-Dumont told Cartier that **checking the time in flight was nearly impossible.**" (Optional: "Both hands on the controls" works for an airship too, so there's no need to say "plane".)
- Slide 6: "The age of flight has begun, and so has the age of the wristwatch." → "Europe's first certified powered flight, by the man with a watch on his wrist." A safer alternative: "Europe has its first flying hero, and he wears his watch on his wrist." Also consider "the first world record certified by the FAI" instead of "the first aviation record ever officially recognized".
- Slide 7: "For seven years only Santos-Dumont wears it. In 1911 **Cartier puts the Santos on sale**, and men start wearing watches on their wrists." → "In 1911 **Cartier puts the Santos on sale.** A watch on a man's wrist starts to look modern, not feminine. The First World War will finish the job."
- Caption: "Before 1904, a wristwatch on a man was almost unthinkable." → "Before 1904, a wristwatch on a gentleman was seen as women's jewelry."

## Optional improvements
- Slide 2: "Watches worn on the wrist were considered ladies' jewelry" → add "mostly", or leave it. It's accurate as a social norm (soldiers were the exception).
- Slide 5: 1904 is Cartier's traditional date and the original watch is lost. It's fine as is; "According to Cartier, in 1904..." would be the extra-cautious version.
- Sources: add https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watch (military wristlets, WWI) and the FAI page. Bob's Watches is a dealer blog, so Cartier's own heritage page or Quill & Pad make stronger citations.

Summary: The core timeline (friendship, 1904 origin, 1906 14-bis record, 1911 launch, screws) holds up. The problems are story-telling overreach: the exclusivity claim, "age of flight begun", and the 1911 launch presented as the cause of men wearing wristwatches.
Apply the 4 required fixes (slides 4, 6, 7, caption) and the post is publishable.
