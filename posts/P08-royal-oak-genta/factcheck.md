# Fact-check: Audemars Piguet Royal Oak and Gérald Genta
Verdict: PASS WITH FIXES

| # | Where | Claim | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cover | Genta sketched it in a single night | ✅ | AP Chronicles, Birth of an icon (https://apchronicles.audemarspiguet.com/en/article/birth-of-an-icon): call on 10 Apr 1970, sketch due "tomorrow morning" |
| 2 | Cover | In 1972 a luxury steel watch was "unthinkable" | ⚠️ (wording) | Overstated: steel watches from top houses already existed (e.g. Patek, Rolex sports models). What was new was a *high-end, gold-priced* steel watch. Acceptable as a hook, slightly hyperbolic |
| 3 | Cover/caption | Steel watch cost more than gold | ✅ (with context) | True vs AP's own gold ref. 5043 (slide 6). Time+Tide: https://timeandtidewatches.com/the-royal-oak-pricing-strategy-in-1972-was-absolutely-wild/ |
| 4 | Slide 2 | Prestige watches were thin, gold; steel for tool watches | ✅ (generalisation) | Consistent with standard histories; interpretive |
| 5 | Slide 2 | AP's agents asked for a steel sports watch | ✅ | AP Chronicles: three distributors (de Marchi, Bauty, Dorot) requested a steel watch "both sporty and elegant" |
| 6 | Slide 2 | "...nobody had ever made" | ❌ (literal reading) | Steel sports watches existed since the 1950s (Submariner, Speedmaster...). AP's quote is "a steel sports watch that has never been done before", i.e. a new kind, not the first steel sports watch |
| 7 | Slide 3 | 1970 | ✅ / ⚠️ | AP: 10 April 1970. Some press says 1971; post follows AP's dated archive (fine) |
| 8 | Slide 3 | Call at 4 pm, eve of Basel fair, AP director, design by next morning | ✅ | AP Chronicles: "April 10, 1970, the Day before the Basel Fair", 4 pm, Georges Golay, Managing Director |
| 9 | Slide 4 | Diver on a Geneva bridge, helmet, eight bolts and rubber seal | ✅ | AP Chronicles: diver at Pont de la Machine, "the eight bolts and the rubber seal" |
| 10 | Slide 4 | Octagonal bezel on paper by morning | ✅ | AP Chronicles (sketch delivered next morning) |
| 11 | Slide 5 | Prototypes in white gold because case was hard to make | ✅ (paraphrase) | AP: monocoque case "so innovative and special" that prototypes were made in white gold (4 ordered from Favre-Perret, 16 Jun 1970). "Hard to make" is a fair but loose paraphrase |
| 12 | Slide 5 | Two years from sketch to Basel | ✅ | Apr 1970 to launch 15 Apr 1972, Basel (AP Chronicles) |
| 13 | Slide 6 | Price CHF 3,300 | ✅ | AP Chronicles; Time+Tide. Other sources give 3,500/3,650 (noted in review_notes) |
| 14 | Slide 6 | AP yellow-gold watch at CHF 2,990, same year's catalogue | ✅ | Time+Tide: yellow gold ref. 5043, cal. 2003, CHF 2,990. Single source |
| 15 | Slide 7 | 490 sold in 1972, record for a single model | ✅ | AP Chronicles: 490 delivered, record for identical units of a model sold in one year (AP's own record) |
| 16 | Slide 7 | Under 7% of AP production until 1976 | ✅ | AP Chronicles 1976–1982: "never accounted for more than 7% of the watches produced... nor 6% of the turnover" (1972–1976) |
| 17 | Slide 8 | Genta's personal steel 5402 with unique gold bezel, Sotheby's 2022, CHF 2,107,000 | ✅ | Quill & Pad: https://quillandpad.com/2022/05/11/gerald-gentas-personal-and-unique-audemars-piguet-royal-oak-jumbo-ref-5402-auctioned-by-sothebys-for-record-setting-chf-2107000/ (Sotheby's Geneva, 10 May 2022, incl. fees) |
| 18 | CTA | "Fifty years later" | ✅ | 1972 to 2022 (50th anniversary); loose but fine |
| 19 | Caption | Two years to build, years more to win people over | ✅ | Consistent with AP Chronicles (#12, #16) |

## Required fixes
- Slide 2: "Then AP's agents asked for a steel sports watch nobody had ever made." → "Then AP's agents asked for something new: a steel sports watch as elegant as gold."

## Optional improvements
- Cover: "a luxury watch made of steel was unthinkable" → "a steel watch priced like gold was unthinkable" (more accurate, same punch).
- Slide 5: "The case was so hard to make" → "The case was so new and complex" (closer to AP's wording).
- Slide 7: "a record for a single model" → "an AP record for a single model" (removes any reading as an industry-wide record).
- Slide 8: could add "in Geneva" and "with fees", optional.

Summary: Nearly all facts check out against AP's own archive, Time+Tide and Quill & Pad.
One required fix (slide 2 implies no steel sports watch had existed before); the rest is optional tightening.
