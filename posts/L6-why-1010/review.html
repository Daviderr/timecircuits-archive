Why watch ads show 10:10 (and why we show 10:04)

Icons & Curiosities · planned Launch #6 · manual post after warm-up · 8 slides

Caption

Almost every watch ad shows 10:10. Ours shows 10:04. 🕙⚡

10:10 frames the logo and looks like a smile, and a 2017 study found it actually makes people want to buy. 10:04 is when lightning struck the Hill Valley clock tower. Welcome to Time Circuits: the stories behind the watches that shaped history.
